Reliable Electrical Maintenance

Don't risk your home's safety with DIY electrical work. Our team of skilled handymen is ready to solve your day-to-day electrical problems, ensuring all connections are robust, compliant, and perfectly functional.


New Installations & Upgrades

  • Light Fittings: Professional mounting of heavy chandeliers, flush ceiling lights, wall sconces, and LED spotlights in any room.
  • Sockets & Switches: Replacing old, cracked faceplates or upgrading to modern sockets with integrated USB charging ports.
  • Security & Sensors: Installing outdoor motion-sensor floodlights, hardwired smoke detectors, and smart doorbells.
  • Ventilation: Diagnosing and replacing noisy or failed extractor fans in your kitchen or bathroom.

Diagnostics & Cable Protection

  • Fault Finding: Identifying why specific sets of lights or outlets are tripping or failing to work.
  • Inaccessible Bulbs & Starters: Let us safely handle those hard-to-reach, high-ceiling bulb replacements or fluorescent starter swaps.
  • Cable Management: Neatly installing trunking (cable channels) to hide and protect unsightly or dangerous exposed wiring.

Safety First, Always

  • Safe & Compliant: We work methodically, adhering to strict safety standards to completely eliminate short-circuit risks.
  • No Mess Left Behind: Dust from drilling? Not a chance. We clean up meticulously after every single installation.
  • Local Promptness: Readily available for residents in Jesmond, Gosforth, Heaton, and Gateshead.

Inspection & Diagnostics

Every job begins with a careful assessment of your wiring, circuits and fixture compatibility so we can recommend the safest and most efficient solution.

  • • Fault detection for flickering lights or intermittent power.
  • • Load assessment for new fittings and extensions.
  • • Advice on smarter switches, energy-saving lighting and future-ready wiring.
