Professional Carpentry & Joinery Services

Our skilled carpenters combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques to deliver exceptional results for your home or business. Whether you need new installations, repairs, or custom woodwork, we ensure precision, durability, and aesthetic appeal in every project.


Door Installation & Repair

  • Door Hanging: Expert installation of internal and external doors with perfect alignment and smooth operation.
  • Door Repairs: Fixing warped doors, replacing hinges, handles, and locks for restored functionality.
  • Custom Doors: Bespoke door construction and fitting for unique architectural requirements.
  • Fire Doors: Certified installation and maintenance of fire-rated doors for safety compliance.

Window Fitting & Maintenance

  • Window Installation: Professional fitting of uPVC, wooden, or aluminum windows with weather-tight seals.
  • Window Repairs: Restoring sashes, frames, and mechanisms for smooth operation and energy efficiency.
  • Sill & Casing Work: Custom window sills, casings, and trim work for a finished appearance.
  • Security Upgrades: Installation of window locks, restrictors, and reinforced frames.

Interior Woodwork & Trim

  • Skirting Boards & Architraves: Precision installation and repair of baseboards and door trim.
  • Picture Rails & Dado Rails: Period-style or contemporary rail installations for decorative appeal.
  • Panelling & Wainscoting: Wall panelling, beadboard, and decorative wood features.
  • Staircase Work: Newel posts, balusters, handrails, and staircase repairs.

Kitchen & Bathroom Fitting

  • Kitchen Units: Installation and assembly of base units, wall units, and worktops.
  • Bathroom Vanities: Fitting of bathroom cabinets, shelves, and storage solutions.
  • Worktop Installation: Precise fitting of laminate, wood, or stone work surfaces.
  • Appliance Housing: Custom enclosures for dishwashers, washing machines, and other appliances.

Furniture Making & Restoration

  • Custom Furniture: Bespoke pieces including shelves, cabinets, tables, and storage solutions.
  • Furniture Repair: Restoring chairs, tables, cabinets, and antique pieces to their former glory.
  • Drawer & Door Replacement: Fitting new runners, hinges, and hardware for smooth operation.
  • Wood Finishing: Sanding, staining, varnishing, and polishing for beautiful, durable finishes.

Quality Craftsmanship Guaranteed

  • Precision Work: Every cut, joint, and finish is executed with meticulous attention to detail.
  • Durable Materials: We use high-quality timber, hardware, and finishes that stand the test of time.
  • Clean & Tidy: Professional work practices ensure minimal disruption and thorough cleanup.
  • Local Expertise: Deep knowledge of regional building styles and requirements in Newcastle and Tyneside.

Our Carpentry Process

  • Survey & planning: We assess the space, measure precisely, and discuss material choices before work begins.
  • Material selection: Timber, hardware, and finishes chosen for strength, style, and durability.
  • Execution: Installing with accurate joints, level fittings, and secure fastenings.
  • Final checks: We test doors, drawer movement, and alignment to ensure everything functions perfectly.
