Total Property Refresh, Inside & Out

Whether you're preparing a property for rent, putting it up for sale, or just want a spotless home, our integrated interior cleaning and exterior yard maintenance service provides a complete solution without the hassle of hiring multiple contractors.


Domestic Cleaning (Interior)

  • Deep Cleaning: Thorough vacuuming, floor mopping, dusting, and full sanitation of bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Surface Rejuvenation: Refreshing the look of all rooms, scrubbing away grime and hard-to-reach dust buildup.
  • End of Tenancy: Ensuring your property looks pristine and welcoming for new tenants.

Yard & Garden Maintenance (Exterior)

  • Professional Jet Washing: High-pressure cleaning to blast away stubborn stains, moss, and dirt from driveways, patios, and paving.
  • Garden Maintenance: Lawn mowing, cutting back overgrowth, trimming, and general clearing of garden debris.
  • Gutter Clearing: Professional clearance of gutters and downpipes to prevent water damage and ensure proper drainage.

Eco-Friendly Cleaning

We use low-irritant cleaners and modern equipment that protect your home, garden, and indoor air quality without compromising on deep cleaning power.

  • • Safe products for families, children and pets.
  • • Waste removed responsibly.
  • • Efficient cleaning techniques that reduce water use where possible.
