Save money and extend the life of your favorite pieces. Serving Jesmond, Gosforth, Heaton, Gateshead, and the rest of Tyneside.

Give Your Furniture a Second Chance

Why spend a fortune on new items when we can breathe life back into the ones you have? We recondition, reinforce, and repair damaged furniture, restoring both its functionality and beautiful aesthetic appeal.


Structural & Hardware Repairs

  • Structural Reinforcement: Consolidating wobbly dining chairs, unstable tables, or squeaky bed frames.
  • Hardware Replacement: Replacing snapped hinges, broken cabinet handles, or failed pneumatic dampers.
  • Drawer Mechanisms: Fitting new drawer runners so your storage glides effortlessly again.
  • Door Adjustments: Fine-tuning skewed kitchen cabinet or wardrobe doors so they align perfectly.

Restoration & Finishing Touches

  • Defect Remediation: Filling unsightly screw holes, repairing chipped veneer, and fixing damaged edges.
  • Aesthetic Touch-ups: Masking surface scratches and rejuvenating worn-out finishes.
  • Enhanced Safety: Adding extra wall-brackets and security anchors to older, top-heavy cabinets.

FastFixes Professionalism

  • Punctuality & Cleanliness: We guarantee an on-time arrival across Newcastle and always leave your home meticulously clean.
  • Fully Equipped: We arrive with specialized tools, heavy-duty glues, and an array of spare screws and fixings to solve problems on the spot.

Repair Standards & Materials

We choose the right fix for the right furniture, using matching finishes and quality materials for repairs that look seamless and last longer.

  • • Timber glue and reinforcements for structural strength.
  • • New runners, hinges, and fixings for renewed reliability.
  • • Veneer repair, filling, and refinishing for surface damage.
  • • Secure anchoring for tall or heavy storage furniture.
