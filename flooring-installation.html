Subfloor Prep & Installation

  • Versatile Systems: Expert fitting for laminate, engineered wood, and click LVT (Luxury Vinyl Tile).
  • Thorough Evaluation: We check subfloor moisture and levelness before laying a single plank.
  • Correct Insulation: Installing the optimal underlay and vapor barriers tailored to your specific wood and subfloor type.

Professional Finishing Details

  • Undercutting Frames: We trim door architraves so the flooring slides seamlessly underneath, eliminating ugly gaps.
  • Skirting & Beading: Fitting new skirting boards or matching scotia/beading to cover expansion gaps cleanly.
  • Door Planing: If your new floor is higher than the old one, we trim your doors to swing and shut perfectly.
  • Pipe Rosettes: Seamless integration around radiator pipes using coordinated rosettes.

Recommendations Before We Arrive

  • Acclimation: Laminate and wood should be stored in the target room for 24-48 hours prior to fitting to adjust to the temperature and humidity.
  • Preparation: Please clear the room of small objects. We are happy to help move heavy furniture if needed!
  • Access: Keep a clear access route for installers, materials, and tools to avoid delays.

Materials & Care Advice

We recommend the best flooring type for your room, and provide practical maintenance guidance so your new floor looks great for years.

  • Laminate: wipe with a damp cloth and avoid standing water to protect the core.
  • Engineered wood: maintain balanced humidity and use felt pads under furniture.
  • LVT: easy clean with mild floor cleaner and soft sweeping.
  • Expansion gaps: leave recommended clearances to prevent buckling during temperature changes.
