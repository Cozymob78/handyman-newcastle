Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our handyman services

How quickly can you respond to emergency repairs?

We offer same-day emergency services for urgent plumbing, electrical, and safety issues. For non-emergency work, we typically schedule within 1-2 days.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes! We offer free, no-obligation quotes for all our services. Simply send us a WhatsApp message with details of your project, and we'll provide a competitive estimate.

Are your tradesmen qualified and insured?

Absolutely. All our technicians are fully qualified, certified, and insured. We carry comprehensive public liability insurance to protect your property.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Newcastle upon Tyne and surrounding areas including Jesmond, Gosforth, Heaton, Gateshead, and the wider Tyneside region.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes, we provide a 12-month warranty on all workmanship and materials. We're committed to your satisfaction and will return to fix any issues at no extra cost.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfer, and major credit/debit cards. Payment is only required upon satisfactory completion of work.
